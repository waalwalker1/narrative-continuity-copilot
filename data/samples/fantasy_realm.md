# The Dragon of Thornridge

## Chapter 1: The Gathering

Lord Arthur Vance arrived at the mountain keep at dawn. Arthur had striking blue eyes that reflected the morning light. At thirty years old, he was the youngest knight to lead the expedition.

He carried the ancestral silver dagger at his belt.

***

According to the ancient law of the realm: Magic cannot penetrate solid iron.

The snow fell heavily across the rocky path. Arthur checked his armor and conferred with his scouts.
