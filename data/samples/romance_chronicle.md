# Letters from Pemberton

## Chapter 1: The Summer Ball

Elizabeth Bennet arrived at the assembly rooms in high spirits. Elizabeth had dark brown hair and a lively wit.

Mr. Robert Darcy observed her from across the terrace, remarking to his companions on her keen expression.

## Chapter 2: The Letter

A courier arrived the following afternoon bearing an urgent message from Netherfield Park.

## Chapter 3: The Winter Solstice

Months later, Elizabeth was described as having golden blonde curls when attending the winter gala.
