# The Secret of Blackwood Manor

## Chapter 1: The Midnight Call

Detective Evelyn Reed arrived at the rain-soaked manor at midnight. Evelyn was thirty-five years old and noted for sharp intuition.

Dr. Helena Cross greeted her at the foyer, claiming she had been in London all evening.

## Chapter 2: The Inquiry

Evelyn inspected the study. Rumor has it that Dr. Cross had been dismissed from the university hospital years ago.

In a strange dream later that night, Evelyn envisioned the missing wills burning in the hearth.

## Chapter 3: The Revelation

Evelyn confronted the doctor in the conservatory. Evelyn was fifty years old now, her hair dusted with grey.
